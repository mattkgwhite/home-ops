Status: **Proposed — not verified as deployed.**

## Architecture overview

The target decision reserves a 16U rack for compute, network, storage, power,
and patching.

## Configuration overview

No authoritative bill of materials, rack elevation, power or thermal budget,
or cabling schedule exists in this repository.

## Deployment notes

Do not procure from this placeholder. Complete the
[16U rack ADR](../../../adr/0002-use-a-16u-rack.md), inventory, elevation, load
calculation, and network design first.

## Upgrade procedure

Plan against rack units, power, cooling, ports, and maintenance access. Update
the elevation before physical work.

## Troubleshooting

Capture device, port, feed, and cable labels first. Preserve out-of-band access.
